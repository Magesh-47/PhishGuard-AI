About PhishGuard

We're on a mission to make the internet safer by providing advanced phishing detection technology to everyone, for free.

Our Mission

PhishGuard was founded with a simple yet powerful goal: to protect internet users from the growing threat of phishing attacks. With cybercriminals becoming increasingly sophisticated, we recognized the need for accessible, accurate, and real-time phishing detection tools.

Our platform combines cutting-edge machine learning algorithms with decades of cybersecurity expertise to provide instant URL analysis that helps you stay safe online. Whether you're an individual user checking a suspicious link or an organization protecting your employees, PhishGuard is here to help.
